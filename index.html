About RadMap · Armenia

Open data. Shared discovery.

A clearer view of our environment, built through curiosity, accessible technology and the freedom to explore.

OUR PURPOSE

Making radiation monitoring accessible

RadMap is an independent educational and citizen-science initiative for environmental radiation monitoring in Armenia. It brings together locally developed stationary stations, portable dosimeters and an accessible map to help people explore radiation measurements in their surroundings.

Our goal is to make data, tools and knowledge as open and accessible as possible, so that more people can understand how measurements are made, ask questions and take part in discovery.

01 / OPEN DATA

Knowledge to share

Clear, accessible measurements and transparent methods help people understand both the results and their limitations.

02 / CITIZEN SCIENCE

Science for everyone

People can explore their surroundings, contribute observations and participate in a growing culture of open environmental science.

03 / EDUCATION

Curiosity into practice

We want children and students to experience science and engineering hands-on: building instruments, testing ideas and discovering how the world works.

04 / TECHNOLOGY

Built to explore

From detector electronics and firmware to mapping, RadMap connects practical engineering with environmental learning.

Free to explore. Open to everyone.

Science is better when everyone can take part. RadMap is a place to ask questions, share ideas and be yourself while exploring the world together.

RadMap is an educational and citizen-science initiative. It does not replace official radiation monitoring or emergency-response systems.
